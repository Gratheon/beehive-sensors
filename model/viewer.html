Beehive scale

A wooden hive stand with one load cell, an ESP32 pod flush in its side and a solar panel that is the landing board or hooks onto whichever side faces south. No loose cables, nothing sticking out, and the same modules fit the Robotic Beehive and the Entrance Observer.
